# Roadmap

- **v0.1 Foundation** — contracts, migration, job persistence, projection engine, CLI, notices, CI and benchmark fixtures.
- **v0.2 Workstation shell** — complete dock/panel persistence, language/theme pack editor, artifact browser and diagnostics UI.
- **v0.3 Unified projection** — production preview cache, automatic resource-aware batching, all media formats, rig validation and benchmarks.
- **v0.4 Pipeline integration** — route every inherited stage through versioned jobs/artifacts and complete crash resume.
- **v0.5 UI polish and usability** — refine the Midnight visual system, hierarchy, spacing, icons, previews and interaction states; complete Persian/RTL presentation; improve high-DPI scaling, keyboard navigation, accessibility and perceived responsiveness; validate the workflow through hands-on usability sessions.
- **v0.9 Public beta** — component downloads, signed updater/rollback, installer, sample scene and hardware matrix.
- **v1.0 Stable** — acceptance suite, reproducible artifacts and proven non-destructive migration.

## UI polish acceptance targets

- Consistent appearance across the import, review, mask, SfM, dataset, training and perspective-export workspaces.
- Complete English and Persian interfaces without clipped, overlapping or unintentionally untranslated user-facing text.
- Correct left-to-right and right-to-left layouts at 100%, 125%, 150% and 200% Windows display scaling.
- Clear empty, loading, active, completed, warning, failure and recovery states for every long-running workflow.
- Full keyboard access to primary actions, visible focus treatment and readable color contrast.
- Cached preview interaction with a p95 response below 150 ms on the reference system.
- A usability review with representative 360° capture projects before the public beta is declared ready.

Post-v1 candidates: audio passthrough, AV1, Linux, plugin APIs, remote jobs and additional language packs. Embedded training remains out of scope.
